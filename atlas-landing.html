What it actually does

Four things Atlas is built to handle well — replace with your real feature list.

01 · LOCAL

Runs on your machine

No cloud roundtrip for your files or history — Atlas reads and reasons locally by default.

02 · TASKS

Automates the repetitive stuff

Hand off multi-step chores — file cleanup, summarizing, scheduling — and check the result instead of doing the steps.

03 · MEMORY

Remembers context you give it

Atlas keeps track of what you tell it across a session, so you're not re-explaining yourself every message.

04 · OPEN

Open source, always free
